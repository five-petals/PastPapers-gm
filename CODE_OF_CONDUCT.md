# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Gambia Papers is intended to help Gambian learners find reliable exam resources. We want students, parents, teachers, volunteers and maintainers to be able to take part safely and respectfully, whatever their background or experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient and constructive.
- Welcome questions and explain project decisions clearly.
- Give useful feedback about the work, without attacking the person.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish personal information, private messages or identifying details without permission.
- Share only papers and materials that the project is allowed to distribute. Be honest about a file's source and quality.
- Help keep discussions accessible and focused on improving the library.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, hate speech, threats, intimidation or deliberate humiliation.
- Discrimination or unwanted sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private information or encouraging others to contact them without consent.
- Repeated disruption, personal attacks or retaliation against someone who raised a concern.
- Knowingly submitting mislabeled, altered or unauthorized materials, or misrepresenting their source.

## Maintainer response

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code. They may remove content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently remove someone from project spaces, depending on the conduct and its impact. Maintainers should explain decisions where doing so does not compromise someone's safety or privacy.

## Reporting a concern

Contact the maintainers privately at **[add a monitored, private contact address before launch]**. Include what happened, where it happened, and any relevant links or context. Please avoid sharing sensitive personal information that is not needed to understand the report.

Maintainers should acknowledge reports promptly, handle them as privately as practical, and tell the reporter what action they can safely share. Do not investigate or publicly confront someone on another person's behalf. If a maintainer is involved in a report, send it to another maintainer or a trusted project steward.

## Scope

This code applies to project repositories, contribution reviews, issue trackers, chat spaces and events organized by Gambia Papers. It also applies when someone is representing the project in a public space.
